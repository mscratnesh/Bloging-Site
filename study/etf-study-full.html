ETF Momentum Study

The same momentum idea as our stock study, applied to exchange-traded funds: each month, own the ETFs that are rising most strongly and steadily, whether that is the Nifty, a sector, gold, silver or government bonds, and park the rest in a liquid-fund ETF.

ETFs are cheap to trade, hard to go bust, and already diversified, so the rotation needs only a handful of holdings and very little trading. This page shows how it did, and how much of the result to trust.
