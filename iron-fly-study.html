NIFTY Monthly Iron Fly Study

One iron fly a month on NIFTY's monthly options: sell the ATM call and put, buy wings 2% away, roll the short strikes once if NIFTY moves 2%, and take profit at 12% of the credit received. The trade is taken only when India VIX is between 12 and 17.

Tested on every NIFTY monthly option price NSE published, with every trading cost charged on every order: slippage, STT, brokerage, NSE and SEBI charges, stamp duty and GST.

Tested 28 Oct 2016 to 28 Sep 2026 · per sold lot · fixed snapshot
