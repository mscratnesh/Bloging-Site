Reading the buildup

  • Long buildupNew positions are being opened while price rises: buyers are adding.
  • Short coveringPrice rises while positions close: sellers are exiting.
  • Short buildupNew positions are being opened while price falls: sellers are adding.
  • Long unwindingPrice falls while positions close: buyers are exiting.
