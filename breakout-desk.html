Entry — Multi-year base

  • Resistance (multi-year high) at least 2 years old
  • CMP above resistance by 0–10%
  • Volume ratio ≥ 1.5×, above 30-week MA

Entry — Cup & handle

  • Cup and handle base with a defined pivot
  • CMP clears the pivot on rising volume
  • Above 30-week MA

Exit — after entry

  • Stop-loss: weekly close below the stop
  • Trailing: weekly close below 30-week MA
