Entry — Breakout

  • Resistance (multi-year high) at least 2 years old
  • CMP above resistance by 0–10%
  • Volume ratio ≥ 1.5×
  • CMP above 30-week MA

Watch — Near

  • Base ≥ 2 years
  • CMP 0–5% below resistance
  • No volume or MA check — confirm before buying

Exit — after entry

  • Stop-loss: weekly close below resistance
  • Trailing: weekly close below 30-week MA
