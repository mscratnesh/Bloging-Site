About the data

  • FII & DII · NSE are the provisional cash-market figures NSE publishes each trading evening for NSE, BSE and MSEI together. NSE shows only the latest day, so this page saves each evening's figures; its history starts when the site began recording (the earliest days have net figures only).
  • FPI · NSDL is foreign portfolio investment from NSDL's daily report, compiled from custodians' confirmed trades by reporting date, back to January 2005. It covers foreign investors only (there is no domestic figure), and includes the primary market, debt and other routes as well as stock-exchange equity, so it won't match NSE's provisional FII number for the same day.
  • Weeks run Monday to Friday and are labelled by their Monday. Years are calendar years. Buy and sell totals are shown only when every day in the period has them.
  • Both series update automatically each trading evening. Institutional flows describe what has already happened; they don't predict where the market goes next.

Sources: NSE (provisional FII/DII trading activity) and NSDL (Daily Trends in FPI Investments).
