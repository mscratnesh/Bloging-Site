Why early prepayments matter most

Interest is charged on the balance still owed, so in the early years most of each EMI is interest. A rupee prepaid then stops interest on itself for the rest of the loan, which is why a small prepayment in year two saves far more than the same amount in year fifteen.

Shorter tenure or lower EMI?

Keeping the EMI and shortening the tenure saves the most interest, because the balance falls fastest. Lowering the EMI eases the monthly budget but saves less. Most lenders let you choose when you prepay; ask for the option you want in writing.

Prepay or invest?

Prepaying earns exactly the loan's interest rate, with no risk. Investing can earn more, but only if its return after tax beats that rate, and it may not in a bad decade.
