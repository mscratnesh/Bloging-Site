Your privacy

Privacy Policy

This page explains what information Let Money Earn (letmoneyearn.in) collects, why, who else sees it, and the choices you have. In short: you can read everything on this site without giving us any personal information.

Last updated: 29 September 2026

Who we are

Let Money Earn is an independent financial education website run by Ratnesh Kumar Singh from India. In this policy "we", "us" and "our" mean Let Money Earn, and "you" means anyone who visits the site.

Information you give us

We only collect personal information when you choose to send it through one of the forms on this site:

  • Ask a question: your name, email address and question, so we can reply to you by email.
  • Request a call (Services page): your name, email address, phone number, message and the service you are interested in, so we can contact you about it.
  • Order a Google Sheet (Sheets page): your name, the email address of your Google account, phone number, your UPI transaction ID and any message. We use these to check your payment and to share the sheet with your Google account. Payments are made in your own UPI app; we never see or store your bank details, card details or UPI PIN.
  • Reviews and comments: the name and text you enter, and your star rating for reviews. These are published on the site after we approve them, so do not include anything you would not want to be public.

If you message or call us on WhatsApp or by phone (from the Contact page), we see your phone number, your WhatsApp name and what you send, and use them only to reply. WhatsApp messages are handled under WhatsApp's own privacy policy.

This information is stored on our own server. We use it only for the purpose you sent it for. We do not sell it, rent it or share it with anyone for marketing.

Information collected automatically

Like almost every website, our web server records basic technical details of each request: your IP address, the page requested, the date and time, and your browser type. We use these logs only to keep the site running, secure and free of abuse.

Some pages remember your settings in your own browser. For example, the Multi-Year Breakout study saves the options you last picked in your browser's local storage. This stays on your device and is not sent to us.

Cookies

Cookies are small text files a website stores in your browser. Our own site does not set cookies for visitors; the only cookie we set is a login cookie for the site administrator. Third-party services on the site, described below, may set their own cookies.

Advertising and Google AdSense

This site shows, or may show, advertisements served by Google AdSense. About these ads:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at www.aboutads.info.
  • If you opt out, you will still see ads, but they will not be based on your interests.

To learn more, read how Google uses information from sites that use its services and how Google uses cookies in advertising. Visitors from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown.

Other third-party services

  • Google Fonts: the site's fonts are loaded from Google's servers, which receive your IP address when you load a page. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • GitHub: some images, such as our logo, are loaded from GitHub's servers, which receive your IP address.
  • Mutual fund data: the Fund Compare and Fund SIP Calculator pages get NAV data from mfapi.in through our own server, so your browser does not contact mfapi.in and no information about you is sent to it.
  • External links: links to Amazon (our books), Telegram and other websites take you to sites with their own privacy policies. We are not responsible for how they handle your information.

How long we keep information

We keep form submissions for as long as we need them to answer you, provide what you ordered and keep a record of orders, and published reviews and comments while they are on the site. Server logs are kept only for a limited time. You can ask us to delete your information at any time (see below).

How we protect it

The whole site uses HTTPS, so information you send is encrypted in transit. Form submissions are stored on our server and can only be seen through a password-protected admin area. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we take reasonable care to protect your information.

Your rights and choices

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and similar laws elsewhere, you can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal information we hold about you;
  • correct information that is wrong or incomplete;
  • delete your information, or remove a review or comment you posted;
  • stop using your information, where you had given consent.

To make a request or raise a concern, email mscratnesh@gmail.com with "Privacy request" in the subject, or use the Ask a question form. We will reply within 30 days. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings at any time.

Children

This site is meant for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has sent us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the site or the law changes. The date at the top shows when it was last changed. Continuing to use the site after a change means you accept the updated policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy: email mscratnesh@gmail.com or see the Contact page.
