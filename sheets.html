Momentum Sharpe Scan

₹500one-time

Ranks the Nifty 500 by steady strength, keeps a 10-stock portfolio and tells you exactly what to buy and sell at each month-end.

What it does

  • Scans every Nifty 500 stock after the close, by hand or automatically every weekday at 4:30 pm
  • Keeps only stocks within 25% of their all-time high, trading over ₹1 crore a day and above their 233-day average
  • Ranks them by average Sharpe (return ÷ volatility) over 12, 9, 6 and 3 months
  • Holds the top 10 and keeps a stock while it stays in the top 30, so you trade less
  • Watches the Nifty 500 against its 200-day average and flags when to move to a liquid fund
  • Builds a month-end rebalance plan: what to sell, what to buy, and how to split the money

Multi-Year Breakout Tracker

₹500one-time

Finds stocks breaking out of bases two or more years old, sizes each trade to your risk and tracks every exit for you, week by week.

What it does

  • Flags weekly-close breakouts above resistance at least 2 years old, with volume at least 1.5× normal
  • Labels every stock: BREAKOUT, NEAR, EXTENDED, SKIP-RISK or BLOCKED
  • Includes a cup-and-handle scanner with its own READY and BREAKOUT signals
  • Allows new entries only when the Nifty 50 is above its 40-week average
  • Sizes each trade from your capital and risk per trade (1% by default), with an initial stop-loss
  • Logs trades every Saturday and manages exits in two stages: stop, failed breakout, breakeven, then a 10-week trailing average
