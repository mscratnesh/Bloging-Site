Google Sheets tools for Indian stocks

Two rule-based screeners.
You set the rules.

Spreadsheet versions of the methods explained in our Momentum Study and Multi-Year Breakout Study. You get your own copy, choose the stock list and every setting, and the sheet does the arithmetic. They are tools for learning and testing rules, not stock recommendations: the sheet shows how a rule scores stocks, and what you do with that is your own decision.

The sheets₹4,999 and ₹500 · one-time

Momentum Sharpe Scan

₹4,999one-time

A screener that ranks the stocks you choose by a risk-adjusted momentum score (return per unit of volatility, a Sharpe-ratio measure), using the rules described in our Momentum Study.

What it does

  • Works on the stock list you choose (the Nifty 500, a wider NSE list or your own), refreshed by hand or on a schedule you set
  • Applies the filters you switch on: trend, distance from the 52-week high and minimum trading volume
  • Ranks the remaining stocks by their risk-adjusted momentum score, with every input shown so you can check the maths
  • Includes two rule settings from the study (monthly and weekly review) that you can change or replace
  • Shows the study's market-trend filter: whether the Nifty trend condition is currently on or off
  • Lets you model a hypothetical portfolio: enter an amount and see how it would be split under the rule's equal-weight method

Multi-Year Breakout Tracker

₹500one-time

A screener that checks the stocks you choose against the multi-year breakout rules described in our Breakout Study, and a journal for recording and reviewing your own trades.

What it does

  • Checks each stock on your list for a weekly close above long-standing resistance on higher volume
  • Labels each stock's chart state under the rules, such as BREAKOUT, NEAR or EXTENDED, so you can study how the pattern develops
  • Includes a cup-and-handle pattern scanner with its own pattern-state labels
  • Shows the study's market-trend condition (whether the broader market is above its trend line)
  • A position-size calculator: from the capital and risk per trade you enter, it works out the size that rule implies
  • A trade journal: you log the trades you choose to take, and it records them under the exit rules you set, for later review
What these sheets are, and are notPlease read before ordering
  1. Tools, not tipsThe sheets apply published, fixed rules to price data. A stock appearing in a ranking or with a label is the output of a formula, not our view on it and not a recommendation to buy, sell or hold.
  2. You decideYou choose the stock list and every setting, and you make your own decisions. We don't tell anyone which stocks to trade, and we don't answer questions about specific stocks.
  3. No return promisesThe studies show how the rules behaved in the past, with their losing years. That says nothing certain about the future, and the sheets can lose money if you follow them.
  4. Your data, your accountPrices come from Google Finance through your own Google account. We don't see your copy, your settings or your trades.
