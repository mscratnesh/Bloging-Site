Google Sheets for Indian stocks

Two ready-made sheets.
The rules run themselves.

A momentum scanner and a multi-year breakout tracker, built in Google Sheets with the scripts already set up. You get your own copy, fetch fresh prices with one click or on a schedule, and keep full control of every setting.

The sheets₹4,999 and ₹500 · one-time

Momentum Sharpe Scan

₹4,999one-time

Ranks stocks by steady strength, builds a ready-to-follow portfolio and tells you exactly what to buy, what to sell and when to hold back.

What it does

  • Scans every stock on your list after the close, by hand or automatically every weekday: the Nifty 500, the whole NSE or your own
  • Leaves out stocks that are in a weak trend, far below their highs or too thinly traded
  • Ranks the rest by how steadily they have risen, with a risk-adjusted momentum score
  • Two back-tested set-ups to choose from, one checked monthly and one weekly
  • Watches the market every day and tells you when to pause new buys or step aside into a liquid fund
  • Builds the rebalance plan: what to sell, what to buy, and how many shares with your money

Multi-Year Breakout Tracker

₹500one-time

Finds stocks breaking out of long-standing bases, sizes each trade to your risk and tracks every exit for you, week by week.

What it does

  • Flags weekly breakouts above long-standing resistance, confirmed by strong volume
  • Labels every stock: BREAKOUT, NEAR, EXTENDED, SKIP-RISK or BLOCKED
  • Includes a cup-and-handle scanner with its own READY and BREAKOUT signals
  • Allows new entries only when the broader market is in an uptrend
  • Sizes each trade from your capital and your risk per trade, with an initial stop-loss
  • Logs trades every week and manages exits in stages, from the first stop-loss to a trailing exit
