About Let Money Earn

Plain-language money education for Indian investors

Let Money Earn is an independent financial education project: plain-language insights on investing, markets and everyday money habits, without the jargon or hype.

Who runs this site

I'm Ratnesh Kumar Singh. I started investing in 2007 and have been through some bad phases of the market as well as the good ones. Today I focus on investing in good companies and earning a steady monthly income from them.

I am a certified mutual fund distributor, associated with Nirmal Bang. I share what I have learned as an investor; I do not give investment advice and am not registered with SEBI as an investment adviser, so nothing on this site is a recommendation to buy or sell. I also write practical guides on investing and money habits, available as Kindle books on Amazon.

What you'll find here

  • Insights: articles that explain investing, mutual funds, markets and personal finance in plain language.
  • Studies: data-driven research on Indian markets, such as the Momentum Study, Gold vs Nifty and the Multi-Year Breakout study, with the method and limits of each explained.
  • Mutual fund tools: Rolling Returns Compare and the Fund SIP Calculator, which work on real NAV history.
  • Calculators: free SIP, lump sum, EMI, tax and retirement calculators.
  • Google Sheets: ready-made sheets that put the studies' rules to work, for readers who want them.

How we work

  • Education first: every article and tool aims to help you understand how something works, so you can make your own decisions.
  • Data you can check: studies use public data, such as NSE prices and AMFI mutual fund NAVs, and explain their method and assumptions, including what could make the results look better than reality.
  • Honest about limits: past returns do not guarantee future returns, and we say so wherever results are shown.
  • Corrections: if you find an error, tell us and we will fix it.
