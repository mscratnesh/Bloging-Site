Momentum Study

Momentum investing means buying stocks that are already rising steadily, and selling them once they stop. Traders have used the idea for about a century, and research across many markets has found that recent winners tend to keep winning for a while.

We put a rules-based momentum strategy to the test on Indian stocks. To keep it honest, every month uses the Nifty 500 as it actually stood at the time, so companies that later crashed or were dropped from the index are included, and trading costs are charged on every trade.
